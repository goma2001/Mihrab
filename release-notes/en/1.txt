• صلاتي 1.0.0 — the first release: prayer times, Qur'an and dhikr, offline-first, no ads, no account. Based on the open-source Mihrab project (AGPLv3).
• The dawn-calm (فجر هادئ) look: deep green and gold, with the dawn sky as the default.
• Android: a floating prayer button above other apps — the next prayer and the time left, even when the app is closed. Turn it on in Settings → Notifications; it needs "Draw over other apps".
• Prayer times by location: Umm al-Qura in Saudi Arabia, the Egyptian General Authority of Survey in Egypt, and each country's own timetable where one is published.
• Arabic is the app's language when the phone is set to Arabic.
